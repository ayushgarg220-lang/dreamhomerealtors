Elite-X at a glance

A refined address for modern luxury.

ELITE-X offers premium 3 BHK, 3 BHK + Study and 4 BHK + Servant Quarters apartments ranging from approximately 1,800–2,638 sq ft. The development spans 5.44 acres across six towers and is planned around generous landscaped spaces, lifestyle amenities and a low-density residential environment.

Prime location: Sector 10, Greater Noida West — designed for comfortable urban living with a strong lifestyle focus.

The project features more than 30 lifestyle amenities, dedicated parking and 3-tier security, creating a complete residential experience for families seeking premium living.
